description: "Gaps stated honestly: uncollected factors, open refinements, and scope limits."
---

Maintaining complete transparency regarding analytical boundaries and data availability is a foundational tenet of Green Auto India:

1. **ICE Engine Block Manufacturing Factor (Open Gap):** Primary empirical Life Cycle Inventory for Indian grey iron casting and machining of ICE engine blocks has not yet been published by domestic foundries. In the comparative analysis, the ICE vehicle is benchmarked strictly on its Well-to-Wheel fuel combustion cycle ($28.72\text{ t CO}_2\text{e}$), and the UI explicitly highlights this omission.
2. **Natural Gas Thermal Combustion Factor:** Thermal energy consumed in battery coating and vacuum drying (Patil et al. 2026) is modeled using the IPCC standard natural gas combustion factor ($0.202\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e/kWh}$ thermal). Specific regional pipeline transmission losses within Indian city gas distribution networks remain an open refinement.
3. **Domestic Precursor Refining Data:** While active cathode material synthesis is mapped, upstream lithium and nickel chemical refining currently reflects global average datasets, pending the commercial commissioning of India's first ACC PLI gigafactories.
4. **Multi-Impact Category Expansion:** The prototype engine quantifies Global Warming Potential ($\text{GWP}_{100}$ in $\text{kg CO}_2\text{e}$). While the database schema is architected to support Acidification, Particulate Matter ($\text{PM}_{2.5}$), and Abiotic Resource Depletion, secondary characterization factors for Indian ecological regions will be integrated in Phase 2.
5. **Informal Scrapping Scrap Quality:** Due to the absence of centralized scrap recovery tracking, an empirical downcycling discount factor of $0.70$ is applied to secondary aluminium credits to account for alloy cross-contamination in informal dismantler yards.
