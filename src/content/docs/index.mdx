### Executive Summary
India's automotive sector is undergoing an unprecedented transition driven by electrification mandates, FAME, and the PM E-DRIVE initiative. However, automotive Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) in India is severely constrained by a systemic dependency on foreign background databases such as ecoinvent (Switzerland), GaBi/Sphera (Germany), and Argonne GREET (USA). These international datasets reflect European and North American industrial realities—clean hydro-dominated power grids, natural-gas-based direct reduced iron (DRI) steelmaking, high-efficiency highway freight logistics, and mechanized closed-loop vehicle recycling.

Applying foreign databases to Indian automotive products introduces distortions of **2× to 5×** in lifecycle carbon accounting:
1. **Grid Electricity:** CEA Baseline Database v20.0 confirms an Indian national weighted average grid carbon intensity of **0.727 kg CO₂/kWh** (busbar, with renewables), rising to **0.90–1.05 kg CO₂e/kWh** at the wall-socket when accounting for 15–18% Transmission & Distribution (AT&C) losses—nearly **3× higher** than European grid defaults.
2. **Structural Materials:** Over 40% of India's crude steel relies on coal-based rotary kiln DRI with Induction Furnaces (**2.7–3.2 t CO₂e/t steel**), while primary aluminium smelting relies on dedicated captive coal-fired power plants (**18–24 t CO₂e/t aluminium**)—underestimated by **50% to 400%** in foreign datasets.
3. **Logistics & Climate:** Congested Indian road logistics operate at **0.055–0.090 kg CO₂e/t-km** for diesel HCVs and **0.140–0.412 kg CO₂e/t-km** for medium/light commercial vehicles (SFC India & TERI, 2026), while ambient operating temperatures of 35–45°C increase real-world EV auxiliary air conditioning loads by **25–35%** over certified laboratory cycles.
4. **Supply Chain Architecture:** An empirical analysis of Indian OEMs (Tata Motors, Mahindra & Mahindra, Bajaj Auto, Ather Energy) reveals that while structural chassis, closures, and pack assembly are 80–100% localized, battery cells remain **98% imported**, introducing heavy international maritime and domestic multimodal freight legs.

**Green Auto India** is an open, modular, ISO-compliant digital platform comprising an **indigenous automotive LCA database**, a **deterministic TypeScript calculation engine**, and an **interactive decision-support dashboard**. The platform incorporates a 4-tier pedigree data grading framework (Grades A to D), transparent uncertainty bands, and dynamic 2024–2070 grid decarbonization pathways from NITI Aayog (Current Policy Scenario vs. Net Zero Scenario). 

In this Round 1 submission, we define the comprehensive theoretical framework across all CMVR vehicle categories (L, M, N), present a hand-verified worked demonstration case of an Indian **40 kWh NMC811 Battery Electric Vehicle (BEV) pack** yielding **20.24 t CO₂e lifecycle emissions (uncertainty band: 16.1 – 26.8 t CO₂e)** over 150,000 km, and outline a rigorous 4-phase engineering roadmap leading to the Grand Finale at IIT Bombay in December 2026.
