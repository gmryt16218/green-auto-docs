---
title: "Innovation, Novelty & Strategic Value"
description: "What makes Green Auto India different from existing LCA tools."
---

1. **Dynamic Grid Decarbonization Modeling:** First Indian LCA tool to move beyond static grid factors by integrating NITI Aayog's official 2024–2070 CPS and Net Zero pathways, eliminating the 30–45% error inherent in traditional 15-year static analyses.
2. **Pedigree-Driven Uncertainty Quantification:** Every result natively computes central, lower, and upper confidence limits derived from the ecoinvent pedigree matrix, replacing misleading single-point estimates with defensible ranges.
3. **OEM Make-vs-Buy Sourcing Integration:** Directly correlates Indian automotive component sourcing patterns (e.g., imported cells vs. domestic chassis) with transport logistics burdens, exposing the hidden carbon debt of overseas battery supply chains.
4. **Transparent "No Silent Fallback" Governance:** Grade D factors and uncollected components (e.g., ICE engine block manufacturing) are visibly flagged with explicit warning notices and barred from calculations, maintaining absolute scientific integrity.
5. **Multi-Chemistry Comparative Analysis:** Natively evaluates NMC811 vs. LFP across Indian thermal ambient conditions, factoring in raw material intensity, battery pack weight penalty, and cooling energy loads.
6. **Open, Modular Architecture:** Scalable by design—adding a new vehicle segment, material, or battery chemistry requires only the addition of validated data rows, with zero codebase refactoring.
